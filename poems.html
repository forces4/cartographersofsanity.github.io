        What We Must Seek


there is a thing that some never find
immune to fast forward and rewind
for it is not ahead, and not behind

it cannot be gained by toil or grind
it cannot be harvested or mined
it ties us together; it is how we bind

it is not outside, but in your mind
found in wisdom that is refined
it is, essentially, Good defined

can you guess what it is?
your answers are timed

it is found only right now
it is the Will to be Kind







the work man does to gain worth 

You were born a short time ago
you died a short time from now
you were time's attempt to know
what and why, when and how
birth, death, in our sights
these states are our rests 
we strive to stand upright 
hearts beating in chests 
aching to grasp the light
so that we pass the tests

It is not hard to lose yourself to negative emotions. You have to develop a toolkit to limit their damage. Remember that you forgive others not for their sake, but for your own. Every life is going to have pain and anguish. A rich man might rage over a wet newspaper. For someone with ALS, it may be losing the ability to blink that brings on madness. These two people would experience the same level of pain, implying to me the true cause of the pain comes from within, not without. You are revving your engine past its limits due to fear and anger. Sometimes that is good, like when imminent death threatens. Almost every other time, it is not called for, and adds to the problem instead of solving it. You got to slow down when you are not facing instant death. Savor the moment, the new challenge you will overcome as you have so many times before, to get where you are. Ask yourself, "If I did the absolute coolest thing here, that I would be proud of and maybe others would learn from, what would that thing be?"
