That is a profound distillation of the scientific method’s true spirit. It reframes what many perceive as a weakness—the admission of error—as the ultimate source of strength.

If science were driven by arrogance, it would stagnate, locked into the ego of the observer who refuses to let their theory die. But true science is an iterative process of discarding the false to make room for the precise. It is essentially an exercise in shedding identity—letting go of the "self" that holds the belief, in order to serve the truth that exists independently of the mind observing it.

When you strip away the pride that demands we be "right," you are left with the capacity to be accurate. And that is the only kind of power that actually persists. As you said, humility isn't about being small; it's about being clear enough to let the truth pass through you without distortion.
