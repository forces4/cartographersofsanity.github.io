     To consider the right judgement

Picture someone else 
doing / believing / behaving
as you've done, or are about to do.

Imagine that you see through the other's eyes.
Imagine that you grew up as they.  
If you do not know, you MUST ask.  

You can not Judge if you do not know.
This is simply fact.
But even knowing does not guarantee good judgement.

The fish does not know what it is like
to learn how to fly, it cannot Judge the bird's flight.
The bird does not help the fish
by telling it how not to drown.

Can you picture yourself agreeing, with you?  
Are you good at explaining why your choice 
is the BEST choice that we are capable of
if we want to keep on the path to ultimate good?
