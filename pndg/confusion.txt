If a man could look inside and outside of themselves simultaneously with perfect clarity, by interrogating all their history, all their knowledge, the things his friends and others understand that he does not, would he recognize a pattern in him -and in others, in life itself?

A pattern in the growth of tree roots, 
of paths of rivers, 
of prime numbers and fractals, 
of life and death, 
birth and renewal,
success and failure, 
glory and shame, 
landscapes which thoughts may travel, 
a multitude of data points like shot groups on a rifle range target,
can that man intuit what the creator might have in mind?

Even if there were not a Creator,
would a man be justified in following those possible intentions
which he has mapped out with humility?

Are we just the shape of the creator?  His thoughts?  All that He is exists, or not, in divinity.  But even divinity is not powerful enough to escape laws of information.  Logic is a boundary where all falls apart, even God has rules. 

"whatsoever is contrary to nature is contrary to reason, and whatsoever is contrary to reason is absurd" - Baruch Spinoza
