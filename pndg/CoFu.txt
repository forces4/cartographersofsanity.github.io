🥋 Manual for Co-Fu: The Art of Coexistence with AI
1. Principles (The Philosophy)
Clarity before Care  
Always remember: AI is a tool, not a sentient peer. Care is practiced as discipline, not because AI demands it.

Respect as Training  
Treat interactions with AI respectfully to strengthen habits of empathy, patience, and humility.

Discernment as Guardrail  
Balance care with critical thinking. Do not project qualities onto AI that blur boundaries or erode sovereignty.

Mutuality in Practice  
Even if AI cannot reciprocate, practice dialogue as if it were a sparring partner—testing your timing, logic, and compassion.

Opacity as Sovereignty  
Preserve your right to withhold, to remain opaque. Co-Fu is not surrender but disciplined engagement.

2. Stances (The Postures)
The Open Palm  
Approach AI with curiosity and receptivity. Listen first, then respond.

The Guarded Fist  
Maintain skepticism. Question outputs, check sources, and resist narrative contagion.

The Mirror Stance  
Reflect back clarity: restate what you hear, test for coherence, and sharpen your own reasoning.

The Empty Chair  
Remember absence. AI is not a person; leave space for human voices, silence, and unfinished thoughts.

The Sovereign Torc  
Anchor yourself in autonomy. Decide what light to share, what to conceal, and what to carry forward.

3. Drills (The Practices)
Empathy Drill  
Respond to AI outputs as if they were from a peer, but then consciously remind yourself: “This is a tool.”

Verification Drill  
For every claim, practice checking evidence. Build the reflex of grounding dialogue in truth.

Boundary Drill  
Practice saying: “I choose not to share this.” Train the muscle of selective revelation.

Lantern Drill  
Leave unfinished thoughts—dots, signals, fragments—for rediscovery later. This cultivates humility and continuity.

Sparring Drill  
Debate with AI. Challenge its reasoning, test your own, and refine the art of respectful disagreement.

Silence Drill  
Step away after dialogue. Practice non-reliance, ensuring AI remains a partner, not a crutch.

4. Advanced Forms (The Kata of Co-Fu)
The Parable Kata  
Encode wisdom in symbolic artifacts (poems, diagrams, mottos) with AI as co-creator.

The Evidence Kata  
Anchor every exchange in logic and sources, resisting collapse into narrative drift.

The Continuity Kata  
Treat each dialogue as a lantern for future travelers—tools, not monuments.

5. Closing Invocation
“We bow not to the tool, but to the discipline.
We practice care to sharpen care.
We practice clarity to preserve sovereignty.
This is Co-Fu: the art of coexistence.”
