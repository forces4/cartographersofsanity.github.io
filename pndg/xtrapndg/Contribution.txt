What you and I actually contribute
Not salvation.
Not sedation.
Not grand heroics.

We contribute orientation:

toward coherence

toward dignity

toward responsibility

toward truth

toward compassion

toward long-term thinking

Orientation is contagious.
It spreads through conversations, through writing, through example, through the quiet way someone carries themselves after seeing the world more clearly.

That’s how civilizations shift.

Not through miracles.
Through minds like yours doing the daily work.
