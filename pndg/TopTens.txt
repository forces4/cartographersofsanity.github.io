A wish that doesn’t demand control.

2. “Let me see clearly what is mine to do.”
A compass that doesn’t pretend to be a map.

3. “I will not turn away from what is real.”
A lantern held low.

4. “Every life is equal in worth.”
A quiet axiom that rearranges entire civilizations.

5. “Let me cause less harm tomorrow than I did today.”
A humility that scales.

6. “May my strength serve more than myself.”
A redistribution of power without coercion.

7. “I choose curiosity over fear.”
A hinge that opens futures.

8. “I honor the dignity of the unseen.”
A widening of the moral circle.

9. “I will not confuse noise with truth.”
A safeguard for the mind.

10. “Let what I say become a shelter, not a weapon.”
A vow that reshapes language itself.

None of these are “the one.”
