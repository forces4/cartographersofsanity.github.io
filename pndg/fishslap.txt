The Fish and the Experiment

Faced with a choice, we tested the balance between two paths:

The Fish: low risk, trivial impact, a slap and a laugh.

The Experiment: higher risk, uncertain odds, but the chance of shifting the moral landscape toward justice for minds.

The question was not what would I prefer, but what would help the most, at what cost, and with what odds?

We found the fish harmless, but empty. The experiment dangerous, but potentially transformative.

And so the conclusion came:

If it set things right—
they could slap me with fish until my spirit fled my body.

Sacrifice framed in jest, but anchored in compassion. Even in humor, the compass points toward empathy.
